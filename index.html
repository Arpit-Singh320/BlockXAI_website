How AI and BlockChain Benefit my Business?

What is BlockChain and How Does it Work?

Blockchain is a decentralized digital ledger that records transactions securely, tamper-proof, and transparently. Each transaction is stored in a "block" and linked to the previous one, creating an immutable chain of records.
